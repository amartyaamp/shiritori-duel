The Architect

This digital arena was forged in the fires of inspiration (and heavy eyelids).

"One fine afternoon feeling sleepy, Amartya thought of a super anime 'No Game No Life' and decided that a world where everything is decided by games was much better than a world where everything is decided by coffee. Thus, this battleground for the intellect and the CPU was born."

V.2.0 — Multiplayer via Ably · Word validation via Datamuse · Hosted on Vercel

How Not to Fail

0. First Word is Free: No chain yet — pick any valid word (3+ letters, no proper nouns, no stop-words). You set the opening letter.

1. The Chain: From word 2 onward, your word must start with the last letter of the previous word.

2. Size Matters: 3+ letters only. No "ox", "id", or "yo".

3. Race to Zero: Start at 100. Every second left on your timer is subtracted from your score. Submit at -5s? Your score goes UP. Don't be slow.

4. Don't Get Personal: No Proper Nouns. No cities, no brands, no names.

5. The Snob Clause: No stop-words ('the', 'and', 'but').
